Qt Multimedia playback backend
------------------------------
The Store package deliberately deploys only Qt's Windows Media Foundation
playback plugin. Qt's separate FFmpeg media plugin and its FFmpeg 7.1.3 shared
libraries are excluded from the production package. Audio/video conversion is
performed by the independently audited standalone FFmpeg runtime above.
